# Camp transportation

Status — Implemented for the bilingual fictional pilot.

Open Transportation setup from the selected camp week in Week setup. This is a separate page so date and housing setup remain manageable. Camp Leadership configures vehicles, named routes, and multiple pickup and return drop-off stops per week. Each stop has a journey (arrival or departure), location, local time in hours and minutes, and optional meeting instructions. One route identifies one vehicle; multiple stops may use it. Own transportation remains available independently for arrival and departure. Actual arrival and departure recording may use another vehicle when staff handle an exception.

Copy transportation from another week appends independent copies of that week's active routes and stops. It does not overwrite the target or connect its future edits to the source. Review copied vehicles and times before sharing them. Copy requests are idempotent, and changes use the week's transportation revision to prevent stale overwrites. Archive and restore retain used arrangements and their history. No deletion is needed to stop offering an option to new registrations.

Public, desk, and paper intake use the selected week's active stops. Each option shows its location, time, route, vehicle, and instructions. Changing the selected week clears the selected stop choices. Printable paper registration lists the selected week's choices. Server-side validation checks the week, journey, active option, and current stop and route revision while holding the week lock. A stale form must be refreshed before submission. A new camp week begins without bus arrangements; staff add or copy them.

Registration stores the agreed location, time, route, vehicle, notes, identifiers, and revisions as a snapshot. Changes to the configured route or stop do not rewrite that snapshot. Show Transportation needs review on Review registration, Check-in, and Departure, and list affected journeys on Transportation setup. Camp Leadership can select a current bus stop or Own transportation and record the staff name and review reason. The change history preserves the previous and resulting plan. The action changes planned transportation only; actual arrival, boarding, and departure records remain unchanged. Family communication remains a staff responsibility; this feature sends no messages automatically.

Check-in offers configured pickup stops and keeps saved or older choices available for reconciliation. Names are not the identity of a stop; two routes may serve the same location. Actual vehicle entry offers the week's configured vehicles and still accepts a different actual vehicle. Departure offers configured return stops and vehicles in its individual and batch workflows, while retaining recorded buses and original planned stops. The roster displays the original plan and any review warning before staff confirm actual boarding and departure.

Migration 026 preserves the original Albrook, Westland, and La Espiga pickup options for weeks already present, with the known morning times. It marks the vehicle for confirmation and leaves return times unknown rather than inventing them. This one-time bootstrap is not rerun for subsequently created weeks. Older registration stop names remain readable and require an explicit review to associate them with a configured route. Fictional setup fixtures include clearly labeled sample bus arrangements and sample return times. The migration does not alter real attendance or departure records.

Apply db/026-camp-transport.sql and db/027-transport-review-audit.sql in order to a hosted database after migrations 001–025. The local pilot upgrades automatically. Tests cover current and wrong-week registration choices, journey validation, hours and minutes, stale changes, copy independence and retries, archival, preserved registration snapshots, own transportation, and actual-arrival preservation.
