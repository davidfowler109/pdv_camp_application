# Staff applications and preparation

Status — Bilingual fictional pilot implementation. No real applicant data until safeguarding, staff privacy/retention, complete role questionnaires, and hosting authentication are reviewed.

## Apply, review, prepare

Corrections — Applicants can permanently delete their own unused drafts, including all uploaded files. Leadership can also delete an unused submitted application before references, review or staffing history exists. Require an explicit confirmation and fresh revision; the server checks eligibility, not just the button. Remove the application, file contents/metadata and private application events together in one transaction. Retain only an application-ID/season/role/actor/time deletion receipt without applicant answers or files. Reviewed, referenced, approved, declined or withdrawn records cannot be erased through this cleanup action.

Applicants can withdraw submitted, under-review or needs-information applications with a reason and confirmation. Leadership can also withdraw approved or declined applications. Retain answers, evidence and history; revoke pastor links and block further submission, preparation, reference responses or approval processing. Show Withdrawn in the queue, filter and private return view. Withdrawal is not a staff deletion or automatic removal from cabin teams. For linked staff, flag a separate leadership review of the staff record and existing weekly/cabin assignments. Show those assignments and require a recorded review before clearing the flag. Existing staff and assignment records remain intact.

Supporting document controls — Use Select a photo or PDF for the field and Upload document for the action. Upload remains disabled until a file and source are supplied. List uploaded files with category, original filename when known, source, Download and Remove document. Staff may remove any erroneous upload. Approved applicants who are not yet ready may view/download/remove only their own non-reference uploads. Confidential reference files and staff-provided evidence remain restricted. Removal requires a confirmation and supports an optional reason. Remove the file bytes and retain a removal event/metadata; removed files no longer download or count toward the active upload limit. If the matching checklist item was approved, clear it, revoke application readiness and flag linked staff for review; do not silently deactivate staff or remove live assignments. Uploading a replacement does not automatically reapprove the checklist. Readiness requires completing any outstanding staff review. Migration 029 adds withdrawal, removal and deletion tracking; restart the local demo, or apply db/029-staff-application-corrections.sql after 028 in hosted environments.

Opening page — Collect the complete About you section (name, birth date, gender for housing, email, phone and address) alongside season, role and information authorization. Save and continue creates the draft, preserves these answers and opens Service and availability directly, without asking applicants to re-enter contact information. Save progress can create an incomplete draft once the minimum identity and authorization fields are provided; its return link opens About you for completion. Existing drafts can still open About you to review or correct contact details. This flow is shared by all three roles and both languages.

Submission confirmation — A successful submit replaces the wizard with a confirmation view and moves focus to its heading. Show applicant, role, season, review status, next steps and the private return link; receipt does not imply acceptance. Submitted answers are available in a read-only disclosure, with no submit control or disabled wizard remaining. This same status view appears on reload or when returning to a submitted/under-review/declined/ready application. Public applicants can finish and return to the camp events website; authenticated leadership can return to the staff application record. Failed submission stays in the wizard with its answers and error feedback. Needs information reopens editing; approved applicants continue with preparation.

Autosave behavior — Background saves do not disable fields, move keyboard focus, change the cursor to busy or insert a banner. A permanently reserved status line reports pending changes, saving, and the last successful save. Edits made while a request is running remain unsaved and schedule another save using the returned revision. Explicit Save/Next actions wait for an in-flight background save before submitting the newest answers. Failed saves preserve the unsaved-changes warning and do not retry indefinitely.

Staff retains Add staff member and the existing directory and weekly assignments. Staff → Applications and preparation opens a 25-row searchable queue, season settings, and a copyable public application link. A Start application heading groups three role buttons: Cabin Leader, Camp Counselor and Volunteer. Each opens the shared form with the role preselected; staff helping applicants use the same buttons. There is no separate assisted entry link. Volunteers express interest in Health, Kitchen, and Program and activities. Public applicants need no Microsoft account. Application roles never grant application login access.

The initial application uses four sections: About you; Service and availability; Church and testimony; Review and submit. Personal details include housing gender, birth date, one email, phone and address. Collect concise experience, skills/qualifications, motivation, testimony, church and reachable pastor contact. Availability selects actual camp weeks by season. Training and closing celebration remain separate preferences, not artificial camp weeks. Counselors must be at least 15 and Cabin Leaders at least 18 on their first selected week. Volunteer minimum age and exact role qualifications remain customer review items; minors require reviewed parental permission before readiness. Health qualification evidence is reviewed separately; selecting Health does not confer qualification.

Creating a draft requires an explicit information-collection authorization, its signer and authority, date of birth, name and email. An applicant under 18 when authorizing needs a parent/legal guardian; adults authorize for themselves. The initial birth date and authorization evidence are preserved. Correction currently requires contacting camp and starting a corrected application; staff must not overwrite consent evidence. No government ID numbers, ID-photo fields, duplicated age/email fields, or social media accounts are collected.

Save progress accepts incomplete application fields. After the first saved draft, changed answers save after a short delay, with saved time and failure feedback. Save and continue remains explicit and step navigation does not require completion. Closing with unsaved answers triggers the browser's standard warning. Submission checks all required fields, relevant age, selected season weeks, truthful-answer confirmation and permission to contact the pastor. Submission freezes applicant editing until leadership requests more information. Applicant-facing clarification messages are separate from private staff decision notes.

Draft → Submitted → Under review / Needs information → Approved or Declined. Leadership records a reason and can reopen a declined application for review. Approval creates inactive staff or explicitly links a returning staff member. No name-based automatic merges. Linking preserves existing names, active state and assignments. Existing staff availability is not revoked merely because another seasonal application is pending. New roles/skills are added after readiness review. Existing Add staff member remains an administrative route, not a replacement for the organization's safeguarding review.

Approved applicants use the same private return link for preparation: explicit health/allergy/diet/medication/accommodation answers with details after Yes, emergency contact, shirt size, minor guardian participation permission, conduct acknowledgment and optional fee questions. For Cabin Leaders, the brief conduct acknowledgment does not replace the full policies; leadership supplies those policies and records that the full versions were signed. Camp Counselors follow the role-specific orientation and actual-rules acknowledgment described below. Volunteer acknowledgments and additional requirements follow the role-specific rules below. Uploading a document does not automatically verify it or satisfy a checklist item.

For Cabin Leaders, leadership separately reviews the pastor reference, appropriate background screening, child-protection training, signed policies, role qualifications and signed parental permission for minors. Camp Counselors use their own checklist below; only Camp Counselors have fee/shirt arrangement review. Volunteer readiness follows the explicit requirements below. Record screening document type, issue date, reviewer and review date and relevant decision, without an ID-number field. A ready decision requires the checklist and applicant preparation answers. New staff remain inactive until ready. This version does not automatically assign weeks, cabins, beds or login permissions. Staff can mark fee arrangements reviewed when leadership accepts an exception; this is not a payment gateway or accounting workflow.

## References and documents

Leadership generates a separate, private 30-day pastor response link for a submitted application, then copies and sends it directly to the intended reference. No email is sent automatically in this pilot. The reference asks identity/contact, relationship and duration, experience with young people, reliability/teamwork/boundaries, concerns, recommendation (Yes/No/Unknown), and the reference's own typed confirmation. A response consumes that link. Leadership may explicitly replace it for a corrected reference; the prior response is retained in audit detail. Applicants cannot read references or internal reviews through their return link.

A blank reference is printable and can be saved as PDF through the browser. A pastor may sign it on paper and provide it directly to camp; leadership uploads a photo/PDF and records source/how received. A signature is one piece of accountability, not proof of independent identity. Staff follow up on conflicting sources. Both direct responses and paper references require an explicit leadership review.

Signed parental permission, signed policy and background documents may be uploaded by approved applicants; signed pastor recommendations are uploaded by leadership only. Files are PDF/PNG/JPEG, have file-signature checks, a 10 MB limit, and a maximum of 12 per application. Downloads and all application/reference/preparation data are leadership-only except for the applicant's own application and the narrowly scoped pastor form. Stored files remain private bytea records in the pilot database, matching the existing local document approach. Production needs the approved private storage, scanning and staff-specific retention implementation before accepting real documents. No camper attachment retention policy is silently reused for staff.

## Private return links and authorization

Applicant links contain a random 256-bit bearer secret; only a hash is stored. They expire after 90 days. Leadership can issue a replacement, invalidating the old link, and help families resume without email. Copyable links are the implemented delivery path; automatic email delivery is a follow-up integration. Do not share applicant links with pastors. Reference secrets use separate hashes and shorter expiry and do not grant applicant access. Reference links reveal only the name, role and season needed to provide a reference. Pages are noindex/no-referrer and APIs no-store; links must still be handled as private credentials, including host access-log redaction before production. Saving uses record revisions and rejects stale concurrent submissions rather than overwriting them. Application actions and document source record an audit history. Current demo authentication identifies the leadership role, not a uniquely named reviewer; individual Entra identity attribution is required before production.

The public workflow is restricted to PDV_LOCAL_DEMO=1, as with the other public pilot forms. JSON and upload request sizes are bounded. Daily draft creation has a basic global ceiling; production also needs appropriate abuse controls. This pilot does not claim a typed name is a qualified digital signature or decide Panama screening obligations. The camp must approve jurisdiction-appropriate screening and minor-serving requirements.

## Volunteer-specific questions and review — agreed November 17, 2026

Volunteer Church and testimony collects approximate years as a believer (0 is valid). Adult Volunteers explicitly answer four questions about relevant convictions, current charges, formal restrictions on duties/contact with children, and previous removal from roles over safeguarding concerns. No answers are preselected. Yes requires either a brief explanation or an explicit request for a private conversation with leadership. Choosing private discussion removes written details; changing to No removes stale follow-up answers. Under-18 Volunteers receive an age-appropriate screening notice rather than adult criminal-history questions; leadership still records their screening review. Age is measured on the first selected service week.

Review and submit includes three individual acknowledgments: prohibited substances/weapons, personal belongings, and admission/permanence following violations. Each must be explicitly checked. Store the exact displayed statements, language, versions, time, applicant and original information-authorization context in the submission evidence. This records acknowledgment and does not claim a verified legal signature. Screening questions remain separate from faith questions and policy acknowledgments. Screening answers do not automatically approve or reject an application.

Leadership sees a screening follow-up indicator for Yes responses awaiting review, with details or the private-discussion request in the restricted application record. Readiness requires explicit Volunteer screening review and notes, qualifications, complete preparation answers, and reviewed guardian participation permission for minors. A change to a screening answer invalidates the previous review and flags linked staff for review while preserving operational assignments.

Volunteer preparation does not automatically demand every generic document. Leadership explicitly selects additional requirements per applicant: pastor reference, background certificate, signed full policy, and training. A pastor reference may be direct online OR signed paper, with its source and review recorded. A requested background certificate requires an uploaded file and staff review; a requested signed policy must identify the policy. The three acknowledgment statements alone do not require a signed-policy upload. Guardian documents apply only to minors. Applicant upload choices show only applicable requested categories; confidential pastor evidence remains staff-only. Staff may add optional evidence without making every document type mandatory. Existing approved Volunteer applications with missing new answers can provide them during preparation before readiness.

Migration 030 adds versioned Volunteer acknowledgment evidence. Restart the local demo to apply it automatically; hosted databases apply db/030-volunteer-requirements.sql after 029. Tests use isolated fictional records and cover explicit answers, details/private choices, acknowledgments, submission evidence, optional document requirements, manual approval, and invalidation of screening review.

Review selection isolation — Switching applicants or receiving a newer application snapshot resets the review panel’s generated private link, checklist draft, messages and revision. Never carry a return/reference link from a previous applicant into the newly selected applicant’s panel. The public link always resolves the specified application ID and its purpose-bound secret, never the most recent application.

Preparation submission — The applicant’s name, requested role and season are shown above an existing editable application. Save progress and autosave retain incomplete preparation. Submit preparation for review validates applicant answers separately from the leadership checklist, records a server-owned submission timestamp and audit event, and replaces the form with a focused confirmation, next steps, private return link, finish/back navigation and Update preparation or documents action. The confirmation persists on reopening the link. Changes to saved preparation clear the current completion timestamp until resubmitted; history remains. Leadership sees a preparation-submitted notice. Submission does not activate staff or mark Ready to serve, and document review remains separate.

Application status consistency — Staff roster rows, status filters and review headings distinguish Approved — preparation pending, Preparation submitted, and Ready to serve. Preparation submitted is derived from the current submission timestamp on an approved application; readiness takes precedence. Saving changed preparation clears the submission indicator. Wizard navigation, including Church and testimony, focuses the new step heading and starts at the top of the page; background saves do not move focus or scroll.

Roster filtering — Changing the status dropdown immediately submits the current name, season and status filters, starting at the first page. Search remains available for typed applicant names and season values.

Unified leadership review — One Save review action saves the checklist, screening notes, a single decision/follow-up notes field, the applicant-facing message and an explicitly selected decision in one transaction. Keep current status is the default; notes can be saved without changing approval. Validate decisions against the proposed checklist before writing either; failed validation, stale revisions or storage failures leave the previous record intact and preserve the browser entries. Review notes remain leadership-only. Persist notes for later reopening and retain audit history. On success update the selected review and roster without a full reload, show Review saved with the resulting status, reset the decision selector to Keep current status, and retain saved entries. Ready records show a read-only checklist and notes summary. Warn on leaving with unsaved edits; require saving the review before document changes or withdrawal. Switching applicants isolates all local edits and generated private links; newer server snapshots update a clean review but never silently overwrite a dirty one.

Counselor-only charges — Camp Counselors (Equipantes) are counselors/senior campers. Show their service fee, shirt price, fee due date and optional payment questions only for that role. Only their readiness checklist requires fee/shirt arrangements reviewed. The shared season editor explicitly labels the charges as counselor-only. Shirt-size collection and safeguarding requirements are separate from these charges. Retain historical answers and audit records without continuing to require fee review for Cabin Leaders or Volunteers.

## Staff availability and assignments — agreed November 17, 2026

Which weeks can you serve? records availability, not a booking or an assignment. Offer actual configured camp weeks. Staffing uses the latest submitted application for that person and season that is Approved and Ready to serve; pending or withdrawn applications do not silently replace an operational availability plan. Leadership can record a confirmed seasonal plan in Staff directory. That plan takes precedence without rewriting application answers. No plan/application means Availability not recorded; an explicitly saved empty plan means unavailable for the season. Seasonal plans are independent and revision checked.

Weekly Health, Kitchen and Program assignment menus offer available active qualified staff by default. Week setup uses the same rule for new Cabin Leader and Camp Counselor team assignments. An explicit exception section offers otherwise qualified staff outside availability or with no recorded availability. Leadership must confirm availability with the people selected and record a reason. The server enforces this even for forged or stale form submissions. Exceptions never bypass active status, qualifications, required adult leadership, capacity or other cabin safety rules. Where a ready application supplies a birth date, check role minimum age on the actual assigned week, including exceptions.

Keep existing assignments when availability is missing or changes; flag them on Staff and Week setup. Unchanged cabin team members can remain while staff correct unrelated settings. Adding a new person or changing their cabin role requires availability or a fresh confirmed exception. Record each exception with person, week, role, cabin when applicable, availability snapshot, reason and recorder. Show availability/change history in the directory. Local demo identifies the leadership role; unique authenticated recorder attribution remains a production requirement.

## Camp Counselor preparation — role-specific requirements

Equipantes are counselors/senior campers, not the cabins’ responsible adult leadership. Their routine readiness checklist is pastor reference reviewed, age-appropriate counselor review, camp orientation (boundaries, conduct and reporting concerns), role qualifications, counselor fee/shirt arrangements, and reviewed guardian permission for minors. Screening review notes describe the checks appropriate to age and duties, with reviewer and date; an official background certificate is not implied. These are readiness checks, not additional application-submission barriers.

Camp-rules acknowledgment is optional and follows the explicit reviewer election below. Do not require legacy rule titles or the former leadership acknowledgment checkbox.

Additional background certificates, additional child-protection training and separately signed policies are required only when leadership explicitly selects them for that counselor. A separate policy must be identified. Requested background certificates require a file and staff review. Applicant upload choices contain only applicable requested categories, plus guardian permission for minors; staff can add other supporting evidence without making every category mandatory. The pastor reference may be obtained directly or on paper. Existing generic screening/training/policy ticks do not silently satisfy the new counselor-specific reviews. Existing Ready staff and operational assignments are not retroactively changed. Cabin Leader requirements are unchanged. Routine checklist fields use existing application preparation and audit storage. Rules PDF storage is extended by migration 032 below.

## Optional camp rules acknowledgment — revised November 18, 2026

For Camp Counselors, the reviewer explicitly selects Require acknowledgment of camp rules. Default is unselected; neither legacy rule names nor existing completion checkboxes opt anyone in. When unselected, camp rules do not block preparation submission or readiness. Cabin Leader policy requirements and Volunteer requirements are unchanged. Uploading alone does not elect the requirement; Save review saves the complete requirement.

Client-supplied timestamps are ignored. Leadership sees Acknowledged by applicant, revision and date automatically; there is no second leadership completion checkbox.

Title, revision, document URL or selected PDF changes require a new acknowledgment and clear the current preparation-submission marker so the private link opens the updated request. Keep historical audit evidence. A Ready decision while waiting returns a specific instruction, preserves all inputs and makes no partial changes. Removing the selected PDF makes it inaccessible, revokes readiness and flags linked staff for review; no operational staff/assignment deletion occurs.

An applicant can download only the selected rules PDF through their own valid private link; other camp-provided files and confidential references remain restricted.
