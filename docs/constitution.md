# Palabra de Vida Camp Application Constitution

## 1. Serve campers and support the ministry

Palabra de Vida is a Christian camp in Panama serving children, youth, and adults. The application exists to help the camp care for campers and carry out its ministry. Prioritize the camper journey from assisted registration through safe departure. Extend the system to other camp operations as those needs are defined.

## 2. Make registration accessible and preserve staff discretion

Parents, guardians, pastors, and camp staff can help campers register. Record who completed the form and who had authority to give permission. Track registration confirmation, permission to participate, physical presence, and payment separately. The camp follows up on fees and tracks scholarship use. Authorized staff can approve participation when a balance remains.

## 3. Keep people accountable for camper care

Camp staff must be able to determine who has arrived, where each camper is housed, which adults are responsible for them, and how each camper departed. The system can suggest placements. Authorized staff can change a suggestion, with the decision and reason recorded. Enforce physical bed capacity and required supervision coverage.

## 4. Collect and retain personal data for a defined purpose

Every personal field must serve a legitimate camp purpose. Leave personal fields blank on new registrations and consent choices unselected. Record explicit authorization for data collection and participation. Use the camper’s age when authorization is given. A parent or legal guardian authorizes for campers under 18. A camper aged 18 or older authorizes for themselves. Record permission to use camper images in promotional interviews separately. Either answer allows registration and participation.

Apply the same privacy and retention rules to all campers. Limit staff access to the information they need for their role. Remove or anonymize information when its approved retention period ends.

## 5. Stewardship of camp resources

Palabra de Vida will choose technology the camp can afford to operate and maintain. Evaluate recurring costs across the full year, including hosting, storage, backups, and staff time. Pay for services when they materially improve camper safety, protect personal information, or reduce the work required to run camp. Keep the system simple enough for the organization to maintain between seasons.

## 6. Keep the pilot flexible

Make routine work easy for staff to adopt. Enforce legal requirements and the camper safety and capacity rules the camp has agreed to. For other decisions, favor visible status, reminders, and authorized staff judgment. Add restrictions after staff identify a recurring problem that a rule would solve.
