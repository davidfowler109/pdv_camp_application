# Sensitive Decisions

This register records policy, rationale, and review triggers. Keep camper records and signed forms in restricted camp records.

## PDV-001 — Who can authorize registration and how the camp verifies it

**Status:** Agreed for the registration design  
**Policy owner:** Camp Leadership  
**Operational owner:** Camp Registration  
**Specification:** [Registration authorization routes](registration-spec.md#proposed-authorization-routes)

### Decision

- Use the camper’s age when authorization is given. A parent or legal guardian authorizes for a minor. An 18-year-old camper authorizes for themselves. Keep data collection and participation as separate decisions. Ask separately about use of the camper’s image in interviews promoting camp on social media. Either answer permits registration and participation. A missing answer on an older signed form does not grant permission.
- Offer email, WhatsApp, shared-device, and paper routes. Ask the signer to identify themselves and declare their relationship to the camper where applicable. For remote requests, send the link directly to a contact reasonably attributable to the signer.
- Camp Leadership designates pastors and staff who can witness signatures. Record a person who saw the signer give authorization as a witness. Record a person who merely delivered the form as the courier.
- Camp Registration records who gave authorization, when, how, which notice version they saw, and who witnessed or recorded it when applicable. Routine cases can proceed after this documented check. Routine registration requires neither ID uploads nor a second verifier.
- If information conflicts or a custody concern arises, keep authorization pending. Camp Registration follows up through an independently established contact or refers the case to Camp Leadership. Request additional evidence when it would resolve the specific issue.

- For signed paper forms, Camp Registration records how the form arrived and who supplied it when known, along with the staff member who entered it. This provides accountability for routine intake. If the person delivering the form did not witness the signing, record them as the source or courier. A photo or PDF of the signed form and the original’s filing location are optional. Camp Registration and Camp Leadership can access the attachment. Apply the 15-month attachment retention milestone.

### Rationale

Families need a workable option without a personal device, email address, or reliable internet. The camp needs a traceable record of consent and a proportionate way to handle uncertainty about the signer’s authority. [Panama Executive Decree 285 of 2021, Article 18](https://monitoreo.antai.gob.pa/api/submissions/538144/files/111652/download)

### Review triggers

Review if the process regularly leaves families unable to register, staff encounter disputed authorizations the procedure cannot resolve, or applicable privacy requirements change.
